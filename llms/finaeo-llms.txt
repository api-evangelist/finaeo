# Finaeo

> Finaeo Inc. is a Toronto-based Canadian insurtech operating a digital marketplace that connects life insurance carriers, independent brokers and their clients. It offers a multi-carrier product marketplace, side-by-side product comparison and quoting, a place-a-policy checkout flow with selected carriers, a white-labeled client portal with digital onboarding and custom Financial Needs Assessments, contact and document management, broker landing pages, and team/distributor white-label offerings. Home market Canada, expanding into the United States via IMO and broker-agency partnerships. Finaeo is a distribution and agency technology vendor, not a risk carrier.

## API posture

> Finaeo publishes NO public API. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/Swagger document, no AsyncAPI, no GraphQL endpoint, no gRPC/proto, no webhook or event catalog, no SDK, no CLI, no Postman collection and no MCP server. No developer, developers, docs, api, app, portal or sandbox subdomain resolves in DNS. Finaeo's own carrier-facing marketing positions the product against APIs: "Leverage a complete no-code toolkit to digitize products, configure applications, and launch custom workflows directly into Finaeo's marketplace. This dramatically speeds up the launch process without the need for bulky APIs." The only documented carrier integration is bilateral and partner-negotiated — a 2021 data handoff into Empire Life's Fast & Full Life Application platform. Everything operational sits behind a broker login or a book-a-demo form. Integration is a partner-gated, sales-led motion, not a self-serve developer one.
